# ecoLoop: Closing the Loop on Community Resources

## Slide 1: The Problem We're Solving

Every day, valuable materials end up in landfills. Design studios throw away wood and fabric. Makerspaces have surplus tools. Schools discard supplies. Communities are fragmented, unaware of nearby resources that could fuel their next project.

**The Challenge:**
- Waste is endemic—useful materials are discarded because there's no easy way to find or share them
- Communities lack visibility into what's available locally
- Current solutions (Facebook groups, word-of-mouth) are inefficient and unreliable
- No fair, transparent way to value contributions and exchange resources

**The Impact:**
Millions of tons of reusable materials are wasted annually while people spend money on new supplies they could repurpose or receive.

---

## Slide 2: Meet ecoLoop

**Trade what you have. Build what you need.**

ecoLoop is a community-powered circular marketplace that turns waste into opportunity. It's a unified platform where communities list materials, match resources to projects, and reward contributors through a transparent credit system.

**What Makes Us Different:**
- **Smart Matching:** Deterministic, exact matching based on material type, quantity, dimensions, and condition—no guesswork
- **Fair Credit System:** Contributors earn tradeable credits, not just recognition. They choose what they want in return
- **Organiser Oversight:** Local organisers verify intake, manage quality, and ensure trust—keeping the system genuine
- **Project Studio:** Full lifecycle support from material sourcing to project completion and documentation
- **Zero Database Overhead:** Runs on JSON files with PHP—simple, portable, and community-friendly

---

## Slide 3: How It Works

### The Journey:

1. **List Materials** – Community members create marketplace listings and offer materials to projects
2. **Find Matches** – The matcher finds exact resource matches based on material specs, location, and community needs
3. **Approve & Hold** – Suppliers approve contributions; quantities are reserved to prevent double-booking
4. **Verify & Collect** – Organisers check materials in; makers confirm collection and complete projects
5. **Reward & Reuse** – Credits are issued to suppliers; they can request goods from the community shelf or use credits for future projects
6. **Share Impact** – Project creators document what was built, creating visibility and inspiring future cycles

### The Economics:
- No cash conversion—credits stay within the community
- Suppliers decide the value of their contribution
- Organisers curate shared stock to ensure demand meets supply
- Circular flow: materials → projects → credits → new projects
